# 1.2.0

- **More proc animations**, each only with its talent, small and see-through like the lightning: **Clearcasting** (Arcane Concentration) a circle of arcane light at your feet, **Heating Up** (Fire) embers rising on both sides of you, and **Fingers of Frost** (Frost) a snowflake turning by each hand. A burst as each procs, then calmer while it lasts, with its seconds. They work in combat. Colour, size, place and strength in the Procs tab; `/magegod procs` shows them.
- **Shield bar for every shield, each in its own colour**: Ice Barrier, Mana Shield, Fire Ward, Frost Ward, a priest's Power Word: Shield, and the protection potions (in their school's colour, 2 minutes). The game only gives the total of every shield on you: with two at once the bar shows both, in the colour of the last one.
- **Pick your armor**: knowing more than one, a click on the armor reminder opens them (Ice or Frost Armor, Mage Armor, Molten Armor if you have it) and puts on the one you pick.
- **Wand swing timer**: a bar with when your wand's next shot comes, lit up as it nears, grey out of reach. `/magegod wand`.
- **Big cooldowns back**: the Blink pulse now also comes for Evocation, Ice Block, Cold Snap, Presence of Mind, Arcane Power and Combustion, each with its own switch; two at once pulse one after the other.
- **Counterspell ready**: its icon while your target casts something you can cut, Counterspell is ready and in reach.
- **Your debuffs on the target**: Fire Vulnerability (Improved Scorch) and Winter's Chill beside its nameplate, with their stacks and seconds, drawn by the game so they work in combat.
- **Group buffs**: in a group, how many are missing your Arcane Intellect; a click casts it on the first one in range, the next click on the next.
- **Reagents running low**: Rune of Teleportation, Rune of Portals, Arcane Powder and Light Feathers, only for the spells you know, and not with the legacy talent Reagent Economy. A merchant who sells one you're low on says so in the chat.
- **Herbs and minerals tracking reminder** out in the world, click to put it on.
- **Gear and bags**: a reminder when your gear is under 25% or your bags have 2 free slots or fewer; a merchant who repairs tells you how worn your gear is.

# 1.1.0

- **Talent reset price at your trainer**: a note under your mage trainer's window says when you last reset your talents, what it costs now, and when the price drops and to what. It shows under the trainer's price too, so you can see whether waiting pays. The game doesn't tell addons when you last reset or when the price drops, so MageGod writes down each reset you pay for from now on and counts from there: a step up with each reset (1, 5, 10, then 5 g more up to 50 g) and a step down for each week since the last one, as players report it. The price the trainer asks always wins. Legacy talents get their own lines. `/magegod respec` says it anywhere.

# 1.0.1

- The Missile Barrage lightning starts smaller (60%) and a little lower, around your character. If you had changed its size or moved it, yours stays.

# 1.0.0

- First release: purple lightning on Missile Barrage with its countdown, a pulse when Blink is ready again, reminders for your armor, Arcane Intellect, mana gem, Well Fed and Comprehension scrolls, an Ice Barrier and Mana Shield bar, a reminder to decipher the scrolls you carry (one click casts Comprehend Scroll on one) and to open your Bundle of Scrolls, a five-second rule bar, the range to your target and an aggro warning. English and Spanish.
