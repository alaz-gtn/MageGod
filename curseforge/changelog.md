# 1.1.0

- **Talent reset price at your trainer**: a note under your mage trainer's window says when you last reset your talents, what it costs now, and when the price drops and to what. It shows under the trainer's price too, so you can see whether waiting pays. The game doesn't tell addons when you last reset or when the price drops, so MageGod writes down each reset you pay for from now on and counts from there: a step up with each reset (1, 5, 10, then 5 g more up to 50 g) and a step down for each week since the last one, as players report it. The price the trainer asks always wins. Legacy talents get their own lines. `/magegod respec` says it anywhere.
